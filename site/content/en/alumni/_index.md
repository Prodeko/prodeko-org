Prodeko Alumni is the association for graduates of Industrial Engineering
and Management at Aalto. It maintains the alumni network and runs events
for its members. There is no membership fee, and every IEM graduate is
added as a member automatically.

## About the alumni

Together with Aalto's Department of Industrial Engineering and Management and the guild, Prodeko Alumni forms the Prodeko community: a network of people from many fields, and a source of support and inspiration for many of us. Newsletters, past boards and the rules are on the alumni site.

## Advisory board

The advisory board brings together alumni, department representatives and the guild board. It supports the guild in long-term decisions and corporate collaboration.

## Lifelong Learning

The alumni's continuous-learning programme: seminars, lectures and mentoring for current students. IEM has an unusually strong alumni network, visible in mentoring and company collaboration.

## Prodeko Ventures

An investment community founded by alumni to back growth companies started by Prodeko members. The Prodeko Seminar in April brings the department, students and alumni together.
