## What IEM is

The IEM bachelor's is a programme at Aalto University's School of Science. In three years you study mathematics, programming, economics and operations management, and pick a minor from any Aalto school.

Graduates work as consultants, product managers, analysts and founders. About half continue to a master's in Otaniemi, the rest go on exchange or into work.

## How to apply

1. **Joint application in March.** Apply at Studyinfo. IEM is its own study option.
2. **Certificate or entrance exam.** About half are admitted on the matriculation certificate, half on the DIA entrance exam in May–June.
3. **Results in July.** Accept your place at Studyinfo and register as attending.
4. **Orientation in August.** The fuksi captains get in touch over the summer. The first week is full of programme.

## Student life

### Haalarit

The overalls are the IEM student's badge: a white boilersuit and a five-colour stripe. Handed to first-years in the autumn.

### Sitsit

A seated dinner party with songs and speeches. The fuksi sitsit are your first, not your last.

### The fuksi year

First-years collect fuksi points from events all year. They pay off on May Day eve on Ullanlinnanmäki.

### Sauna and sports

The guild has a weekly sauna slot. Floorball, football and a ski trip in spring.

## Frequently asked

<details class="faq-item" open>
  <summary>Do I have to join the guild?</summary>
  <p class="answer">No, but you should. Membership is 8 € per academic year and gives access to events, the guild room and member prices.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>How much maths is there?</summary>
  <p class="answer">A lot in the first year: calculus, linear algebra and probability. From the second year the emphasis shifts to applying it.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>Can I come if I can't code?</summary>
  <p class="answer">Yes. First-year courses start from the basics and assume no prior experience.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>What are overall patches?</summary>
  <p class="answer">Badges you get from events, excursions and other guilds, sewn onto your overalls. The unwritten rule: the more, the better.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
  <summary>Can exchange students join?</summary>
  <p class="answer">Yes. Exchange students are welcome at every event, and the international committee runs its own programme in English.</p>
</details>
