PTER (Prodekolaistoiminnan edistämisrahasto) is a sum set aside in the guild's budget every year to support Prodeko members' projects, events, purchases and other activity in all its forms. You can apply for PTER money to organise an event for guild members, to buy something the guild room is missing, or to suggest a magazine the guild should subscribe to, for example. The board always handles PTER applications at the following week's meeting.

[Apply for PTER money through the contact form](https://forms.gle/zM9HofbL4TxWHc1j7)

## Applying for PTER money

### The usual way

1. Write your application through the [contact form](https://forms.gle/zM9HofbL4TxWHc1j7). Under "Keneen haluat olla yhteydessä", choose **PTER-asiat**.
2. Say what the money is for and why. Tell us the amount you are applying for, and add a link to the product or any other relevant details. Add your contact details!
3. Your application is handled at a board meeting, and we will get in touch with you.
4. If the application is approved, make the purchase or carry out the project or event as applied, and claim the costs back through [Prodeko's expense claim form](https://prodeko.kululaskut.fi) with PTER-raha as the allocation. Great!

### Anonymously

If for some reason you would rather not leave your contact details, you can also send a PTER application anonymously. The contact form does not store the sender's details unless you write them in the message.

If you cannot or would rather not carry out your idea alone, and it would be done together with the board or other Prodeko officials, we agree on the arrangements with you separately once it is approved.

In these cases, note that carrying out the idea also takes the board's time, which may affect whether the application is approved.
