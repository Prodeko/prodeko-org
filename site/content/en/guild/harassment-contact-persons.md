Prodeko has four harassment contact persons: Aino Salmi, Kalle Saarivuori, Jenni Palukka & Elli Lahtinen. The harassment contact persons are there for the guild members and they provide a low-threshold opportunity to report about experienced or witnessed harassment. The harassment contact persons can help the harassed to get in touch with professionals and make sure the situation is addressed within the guild. You can get in touch with the harassment contact persons even if you want to talk about the inappropriate behaviour you witnessed. Contact with the harassment contact persons is confidential and will not lead to further action unless the contact maker so wishes. You can find information about Prodeko's harassment contact persons below. You can also contact Prodeko's harrasment contact persons anonymously here:

[HÄRYCHAT](https://harychat.prodeko.org)
