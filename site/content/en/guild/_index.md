Prodeko was founded in 1966 as a subsection of the Mechanical Engineers' Guild
at what was then Helsinki University of Technology, for the students majoring
in industrial engineering and management. In 1989 Prodeko became a guild of its
own. Together with Aalto University's Department of Industrial Engineering and
Management and with Prodeko Alumni we make up the Prodeko community.

## Board positions

The board is elected each autumn at the electoral assembly and runs the guild for a calendar year. Twelve members sit on it.

<dl class="def-list">
  <div class="row"><dt>President</dt><dd>Coordinates the guild and represents it in AYY, the alumni and the advisory council.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Vice President</dt><dd>Supports the President; the seminar, harassment contact persons and officials' wellbeing.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Minister of Excursions</dt><dd>Excursions, longer excursion trips and the corporate relations team.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Captain of Freshmen Education</dt><dd>Introducing freshmen to the guild, freshman events and tutor operations.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Captain of Master and International Students</dt><dd>Master's freshmen, exchange students and advocacy for international students.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Minister of Media and Information Technology</dt><dd>Communications, the Prodeko brand, the member register and board minutes.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Minister of Studies</dt><dd>Advocacy, contact with university governance and the study division.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Treasurer</dt><dd>Bookkeeping, the budget, the financial statement and investments.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Minister of Corporate Relations</dt><dd>The Prodeko Network, corporate contracts and the corporate relations team.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Minister of Internal and Cultural Affairs</dt><dd>Guild events, traditions and the culture division.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Host</dt><dd>Events, the annual ball, the warehouse, keys and room reservations.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Minister of Foreign Affairs</dt><dd>International cooperation, ESTIEM and the external relations division.</dd></div>
</dl>

## Official positions

Officials run the events, the sports, the culture and the international activities as volunteers. Any member can apply, and the first year is the easiest time to start.

<dl class="def-list">
  <div class="row"><dt>Event Committee</dt><dd>The host team, songleaders and the song book committee.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Freshman Division</dt><dd>The tutor responsible and the tutors for freshmen, master's and exchange students.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Media Division</dt><dd>The web team, communications, high school team and Proleko's editorial team.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Guild Room Committee</dt><dd>The guild room, gym, billiards, historians and the store.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Terrace Committee</dt><dd>Looking after the terrace and the events held there.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Culture Division</dt><dd>The guild's culture events.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Seminar Committee</dt><dd>Organising the Prodeko Seminar.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Annual Ball Committee</dt><dd>Organising the annual ball.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Prodeko 60 Committee</dt><dd>Preparations and events for the anniversary year.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Corporate Relations Team</dt><dd>Summer job evenings, the summer job guide and recruiting forums.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>External Relations Division</dt><dd>ESTIEM, bar crawl trips, Tutanic and international events.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Study Division</dt><dd>Mentoring, major responsibles and the hallopeds.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>ÄnnÄ Committee</dt><dd>Recreation for the guild's Nth-year students.</dd></div>
</dl>

## Guild meetings

The guild meets at least twice a year and every member has a vote. Additional assemblies are called when the board or a tenth of the members ask for one.

<dl class="def-list">
  <div class="row"><dt>Annual Assembly</dt><dd>February–March · the annual report, the financial statement and discharge.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt>Electoral Assembly</dt><dd>October–November · next year's board, committees and officials.</dd></div>
</dl>

## Joining the guild

All undergraduate and master's students of Industrial Engineering and Management at Aalto University who have paid the membership fee can be approved as actual members. New students need not join before their studies begin: orientation week gives everybody a chance to pay the fee.

<dl class="def-list">
  <div class="row"><dt>Membership 8 €</dt><dd>Per academic year. Applications open after June for the coming academic year.</dd></div>
  <div class="row"><dt><a href="https://membership.prodeko.org/apply" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Membership application</a></dt><dd>membership.prodeko.org/apply · pay by card.</dd></div>
</dl>
