Prodeko's members are around 900 Industrial Engineering and Management
students who go on to consulting, industry, growth companies and finance.
We build a collaboration that fits your goals.

98% of them are employed within a year. The guild has 6 annual partners and
runs 40+ company events a year.

## Ways to work with us

### [Excursion](/en/yrityssuhteet/excursions/)

Prodeko organises excursions, or company visits, every week. We bring 20–40 students to your office. You provide an introduction, a conversation and something to eat. Book a company visit: [excumestari@prodeko.org](mailto:excumestari@prodeko.org).

### Case evening

We solve your real problem in three hours. The best way to see how the students think.

### [Recruitment messaging](/en/yrityssuhteet/recruiting/)

Summer jobs and thesis positions in the weekly letter, on Telegram and on this site.

### [Annual partnership](/en/yrityssuhteet/prodeko-network/)

Visibility in all guild communications and at the annual ball, plus first pick of events through the year.
